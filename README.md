The funniest excuse a dog could give after being caught eating the homework — an entry for make.fun's "The Dog Ate My Homework: Funniest Excuse" bounty ($2). The program is fully deterministic: no network, no randomness, it writes the same 136 characters every run.

## The entry

> I didn't eat your homework — I upheld the law. In dog law, anything left on the floor is mine, and your essay was trespassing on my rug.

The angle: the dog is not sorry at all — it was enforcing the law. In dog law, anything left on the floor belongs to the dog, so the essay was trespassing on its rug and got claimed. The joke grows out of a dog's own condition (floor = mine, confident innocence) and the punch lands in the last few words. No cat, no taste of paper, no "good boy" — a fresh turn.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts        # writes /output/entry.txt
```

Requires Node 22.18+ (the grader runs Node 22.23.4). `main.ts` validates the text before writing: exactly one line, 1–200 UTF-8 characters, not only whitespace.
